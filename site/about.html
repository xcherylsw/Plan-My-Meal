About Today Cook What

A simple meal planner for households that cook at home and are tired of deciding "what to eat today" every single day.

Why it exists

Planning meals is mostly small decisions repeated forever: what to cook, who's eating, what's already in the fridge, what to buy. Most of that can be worked out for you. Today Cook What does the repetitive part and leaves the choices that matter, like swapping a dish or changing the number of people, to you.

What's inside

  • Meal plans for a day, a week or a month, across breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • A shopping list that subtracts what you already have, grouped by type (vegetables, meat, dry goods) to make shopping quicker.
  • A kitchen inventory with "use soon" flags, so food gets eaten before it's wasted.
  • Per-meal headcounts, so lunch for two and dinner for five each get the right amounts.
  • Household settings for halal, vegetarian and soft-food needs, plus ingredients to avoid.
  • Your own recipes saved next to the built-in ones.
  • Family sharing, so everyone sees the same plan and list.

How your information is handled

You can use Today Cook What without an account; your plans then stay in your own browser. If you sign in with Google, your plans, kitchen and recipes are saved to your household so you can open them on any device. Only members of a household can see its data. The full details are in the privacy policy.
