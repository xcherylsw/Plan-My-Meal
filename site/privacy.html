Privacy policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

Today Cook What ("we", "the site") is a free meal-planning website run by an individual. This page explains what we store, why, and your choices. Questions: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

1. Using the site without an account

If you don't sign in, everything you enter (household settings, kitchen items, recipes, meal plans) is saved only in your own browser using local storage. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

2. Signing in with Google

If you choose "Sign in", Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture, and gives us an account ID. We use these only to recognise you and to show who is in your household.

Once signed in, we store the following so it can follow you across devices: your household settings (such as number of people, diets and disliked ingredients), kitchen items, saved recipes, meal plans, shopping list progress, and which household you belong to. This is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), in a data centre we selected in Singapore.

3. Households

Everyone starts in their own private household. If you share your household's code and someone joins, every member can see and change everything in that household, and can see the names of the other members. To join, a person needs both the code and an invitation: the household owner adds the email address of each family member they want to allow, and we store those email addresses as part of the household. Share the code only with people you want to include. You can leave a household at any time from the account menu.

4. Advertising

This site uses, or may in future use, Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where the law allows. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are used. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage your ad preferences at adssettings.google.com. We do not give advertisers your meal plans, recipes or household data.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep your data on your device and to keep you signed in. Google sign-in and advertising may set their own cookies.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

Household data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To have your account data and household removed, email us from the address you sign in with. Signing out removes the data from that device, but not from your household in the cloud.

7. Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
